# Retirement Runway

Retirement is a mathematical equation, not an age[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span). You achieve Financial Independence (FI) when the passive income from your portfolio exceeds your living expenses[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span).

## The 25x Rule (The Target)
To determine your FI number, calculate your projected annual expenses in retirement and multiply by 25[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span).
* *Example:* If you need $80,000 a year to live comfortably, your FI target is $2,000,000[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span).

## The 4% Rule (The Execution)
The 25x rule is the inverse of the 4% Safe Withdrawal Rate[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span). Historical modeling suggests that if you withdraw 4% of your portfolio's value in year one of retirement, and adjust that withdrawal amount for inflation each subsequent year, you have a highly statistically significant chance of not running out of money over a 30-year period[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span).

## Compounding Mechanics
Time in the market is the heaviest lever you can pull[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span).
* A $10,000 investment growing at an annualized 7% doubles roughly every 10 years[span_38](start_span)[span_38](end_span).
* Delaying your investment journey by 10 years cuts your terminal wealth in half, requiring you to save twice as much later in life just to catch up[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span).
