# Tax Efficiency

It is not what you make; it is what you keep[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span). The IRS provides legal frameworks to shelter your capital[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span). Use them in the correct sequence[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

## The Account Sequencing Protocol
Fund your accounts in this specific order to maximize tax advantages[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span):

<AccountSequencingTool />

1. **The Employer Match:** Contribute to your 401(k) or 403(b) exactly up to the company match[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span). This is an immediate 100% return on your money[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).
2. **Health Savings Account (HSA):** If you are on a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP), max out your HSA[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span). It is triple-tax-advantaged (tax-deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals for medical)[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span). 
3. **Roth IRA:** Max out your Roth IRA[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span). You pay taxes on the seed, but the harvest is permanently tax-free[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).
4. **Max the 401(k):** Go back to your employer plan and fill it to the IRS maximum to reduce your current year taxable income[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).
5. **Taxable Brokerage:** Once all tax-advantaged space is filled, route excess cash to a standard brokerage account[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).

### The HSA Receipt-Tracking Strategy
Turn your HSA into a shadow retirement account by leveraging the IRS loophole that places no time limit on medical reimbursements.
* **The Execution:** Pay for all out-of-pocket medical expenses (co-pays, prescriptions, dental) using your standard checking account, leaving the HSA funds completely untouched to compound over decades in the market.
* **The Archive:** Scan and save every medical receipt in a dedicated cloud folder (e.g., Google Drive or a secure Notion database).
* **The Harvest:** In 20 or 30 years, when you need cash for retirement living expenses, you can submit decades' worth of accumulated receipts to your HSA. You will receive a massive, lump-sum reimbursement completely tax-free, while having allowed the underlying capital to grow uninterrupted for the duration of your career.

## Asset Location
Hold tax-inefficient assets (like bonds or high-dividend REITs) inside your IRA or 401(k)[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span). Hold tax-efficient assets (like broad market ETFs such as SPY or QQQ) in your taxable brokerage[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).
