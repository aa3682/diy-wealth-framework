# Tax Efficiency

It is not what you make; it is what you keep[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span). The IRS provides legal frameworks to shelter your capital[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span). Use them in the correct sequence[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).

## The Account Sequencing Protocol
Fund your accounts in this specific order to maximize tax advantages[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span):
1. **The Employer Match:** Contribute to your 401(k) or 403(b) exactly up to the company match[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span). This is an immediate 100% return on your money[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).
2. **Health Savings Account (HSA):** If you are on a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP), max out your HSA[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span). It is triple-tax-advantaged (tax-deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals for medical)[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span). Pay out-of-pocket for medical expenses now and let the HSA compound[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
3. **Roth IRA:** Max out your Roth IRA[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span). You pay taxes on the seed, but the harvest is permanently tax-free[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
4. **Max the 401(k):** Go back to your employer plan and fill it to the IRS maximum to reduce your current year taxable income[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span).
5. **Taxable Brokerage:** Once all tax-advantaged space is filled, route excess cash to a standard brokerage account[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span).

## Asset Location
Hold tax-inefficient assets (like bonds or high-dividend REITs) inside your IRA or 401(k)[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span). Hold tax-efficient assets (like broad market ETFs such as SPY or QQQ) in your taxable brokerage[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span).
