# Financial Defense

Offense wins games, but defense wins championships. Before deploying capital into the markets, you must build a financial moat to protect against unexpected shocks[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).

## 1. The Emergency Reserve
Do not invest long-term capital until you have a cash buffer. Forced liquidation of volatile assets during a downturn destroys wealth[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
* **The Target:** 3 to 6 months of absolute baseline living expenses[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
* **The Vehicle:** A High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA) or a money market fund. Do not chase yield by taking on risk here; this money is for liquidity, not growth[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

## 2. High-Interest Debt Elimination
Carrying credit card debt at 20%+ APY mathematically neutralizes any investment gains you might make[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

* **The Avalanche Method (Recommended):** Rank debts by interest rate from highest to lowest. Pay the minimum on all, and aggressively route all excess cash to the highest-rate balance. This is mathematically optimal[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
* **The Rule:** Any debt with an interest rate above 6% is a financial emergency. Clear it before investing beyond your employer match[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

## 3. Essential Insurance
Protect against catastrophic loss. You are transferring unrecoverable risk to an insurance company[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **Life Insurance:** Buy Term and Invest the Difference (BTID). If you have dependents, secure a 20- or 30-year term policy covering 10-15x your income. Avoid whole life or universal life policies; they are expensive and mix insurance with sub-par investment vehicles[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
* **Disability Insurance:** Your ability to earn an income is your largest asset. Secure own-occupation long-term disability coverage[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
