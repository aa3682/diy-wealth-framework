# Financial Defense

Offense wins games, but defense wins championships. Before deploying capital into the markets, you must build a financial moat to protect against unexpected shocks.

## 1. The Emergency Reserve
Do not invest long-term capital until you have a cash buffer. Forced liquidation of volatile assets during a downturn destroys wealth.
* **The Target:** 3 to 6 months of absolute baseline living expenses.
* **The Vehicle:** A High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA) or a money market fund. Do not chase yield by taking on risk here; this money is for liquidity, not growth.

## 2. High-Interest Debt Elimination
Carrying credit card debt at 20%+ APY mathematically neutralizes any investment gains you might make.
* **The Avalanche Method (Recommended):** Rank debts by interest rate from highest to lowest. Pay the minimum on all, and aggressively route all excess cash to the highest-rate balance. This is mathematically optimal.
* **The Rule:** Any debt with an interest rate above 6% is a financial emergency. Clear it before investing beyond your employer match.

## 3. Essential Insurance
Protect against catastrophic loss. You are transferring unrecoverable risk to an insurance company.
* **Life Insurance:** Buy Term and Invest the Difference (BTID). If you have dependents, secure a 20- or 30-year term policy covering 10-15x your income. Avoid whole life or universal life policies; they are expensive and mix insurance with sub-par investment vehicles.
* **Disability Insurance:** Your ability to earn an income is your largest asset. Secure own-occupation long-term disability coverage.
