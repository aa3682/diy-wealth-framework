# Wealth Accumulation

Investing is not about picking the right stock; it is about capturing the broad growth of human productivity over decades. 

## The Hidden Cost of Fees
The financial services industry is built on extracting a percentage of your assets under management (AUM). While a 1% annual fee sounds small, it does not just take 1% of your returns—it permanently removes that capital from your compounding engine. 

Over a 30-year horizon, that gap compounds into a large share of the ending balance. Run your own numbers below:

## Core Portfolio Construction
To avoid fee drag, the framework's core is built from low-cost, highly liquid index funds with Expense Ratios under 0.10%. 
* **Equities:** Broad-market ETFs form the core equity exposure. Funds tracking the S&P 500 or the Nasdaq-100 provide instant diversification across major U.S. sectors and technology leaders.
* **Fixed Income:** Depending on age and risk tolerance, bond ETFs are blended in to lower the portfolio's stock exposure.

## Satellite Positions and Digital Assets
Once the core is established, the framework caps speculative or sector-specific positions at 1-5% of the total portfolio. 
* **Digital Asset Exposure:** For cryptocurrency, the framework uses regulated, liquid vehicles rather than self-custody spot holdings, for those who prefer structural simplicity. The allocation is capped at 5%, carved from the equity side of the core model. Spot bitcoin and ether ETFs hold that exposure inside a traditional brokerage account.

## The Automation Rule
The framework does not try to time the market. A brokerage auto-invest buys a fixed dollar amount of the chosen ETFs on the 1st and 15th of every month, regardless of market news.
