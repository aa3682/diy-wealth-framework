# Retirement Modeling

Visualizing the decumulation phase requires dynamic cash flow modeling and withdrawal rate stress-testing.

## Core Frameworks

- **Withdrawal Rates:** Evaluating the 4% rule against dynamic spending models.
- **Sequence of Returns Risk:** Mitigating early retirement drawdowns through cash buffers and bond tents.
- **Social Security Optimization:** Analyzing crossover points for delayed filing.
