# Legacy Mechanics

If you die without a plan, the state has one for you—and it is slow, public, and expensive[span_42](start_span)[span_42](end_span). Basic estate planning ensures your wealth transfers exactly how you intend[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span).

## 1. Beneficiary Designations (The Override)
Accounts with named beneficiaries bypass probate entirely[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span). This is the fastest way to transfer wealth[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span).
* **Action:** Log into every bank, brokerage, and 401(k) account today[span_46](start_span)[span_46](end_span). Ensure you have named Primary and Contingent beneficiaries[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span).
* **POD/TOD:** Add a Payable-On-Death (POD) or Transfer-On-Death (TOD) designation to your checking, savings, and taxable brokerage accounts[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span).
* *Warning:* Beneficiary designations trump whatever is written in your will[span_49](start_span)[span_49](end_span). Keep them updated after major life events (marriage, divorce, children)[span_50](start_span)[span_50](end_span).

## 2. The Core Documents
Every adult needs these three documents, regardless of net worth[span_51](start_span)[span_51](end_span):
* **Last Will and Testament:** Dictates the distribution of assets not covered by TOD/POD, and crucially, names guardians for minor children[span_52](start_span)[span_52](end_span).
* **Durable Financial Power of Attorney:** Appoints someone to handle your finances, pay bills, and manage assets if you are incapacitated but still alive[span_53](start_span)[span_53](end_span).
* **Advance Healthcare Directive:** Names a proxy to make medical decisions on your behalf and outlines your wishes for end-of-life care[span_54](start_span)[span_54](end_span).
