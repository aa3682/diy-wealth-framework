# Tax Efficiency

It is not what you make; it is what you keep. The IRS provides legal frameworks to shelter your capital. This framework uses them in a set sequence.

## The Account Sequencing Protocol

<Callout type="warning" emoji="⚠️">
  **Execution Note:** This framework captures the full employer 401(k) match (Step 1) before routing any capital to a taxable brokerage. A match is employer money added on top of each contribution, before any market return.
</Callout>

The framework funds accounts in this order to maximize tax advantages:
1. **The Employer Match:** Contributions to the 401(k) or 403(b) go exactly up to the company match. Under a 100% match, each matched dollar is doubled the day it is contributed, subject to the plan's vesting schedule.
2. **Health Savings Account (HSA):** For those on a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP), the HSA is filled to the IRS maximum. It is triple-tax-advantaged (tax-deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals for medical). 
3. **Roth IRA:** The Roth IRA is filled next. Taxes are paid on the seed, and qualified withdrawals of the harvest are tax-free.
4. **Max the 401(k):** The employer plan is then filled to the IRS maximum, reducing current-year taxable income.
5. **Taxable Brokerage:** Once all tax-advantaged space is filled, excess cash goes to a standard brokerage account.

## High-Income Strategies

### The Backdoor Roth IRA
If your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) exceeds the IRS limits to contribute directly to a Roth IRA, the "Backdoor" method is the route this framework covers. A non-deductible contribution goes to a Traditional IRA, is held in cash, and is converted to a Roth IRA shortly after.

However, if you have *any* existing pre-tax money in a Traditional, SEP, or SIMPLE IRA, the IRS applies the **Pro-Rata Rule**. You cannot choose to only convert the new after-tax money; the IRS forces you to convert a proportional mix, triggering an unexpected tax bill.

### The HSA Receipt-Tracking Strategy
Turn your HSA into a shadow retirement account by leveraging the IRS loophole that places no time limit on medical reimbursements.
* **The Execution:** Out-of-pocket medical expenses (co-pays, prescriptions, dental) are paid from a standard checking account, leaving the HSA funds untouched and invested for decades.
* **The Archive:** Every medical receipt is scanned and saved in a dedicated cloud folder (e.g., Google Drive or a secure Notion database).
* **The Harvest:** Years later, the saved receipts can be submitted to the HSA for reimbursement. There is currently no deadline, and reimbursement of qualified medical expenses incurred after the HSA was opened is tax-free, while the underlying capital stayed invested in the meantime.

## Asset Location
Asset location places tax-inefficient assets (like bonds or high-dividend REITs) inside the IRA or 401(k), and tax-efficient assets (like broad market ETFs) in the taxable brokerage.
