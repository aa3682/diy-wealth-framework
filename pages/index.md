# The DIY Wealth Framework

Welcome to the open-source playbook for self-directed financial planning[span_56](start_span)[span_56](end_span). This framework is designed to give you the exact systems, calculators, and strategies needed to build and manage your own wealth architecture[span_57](start_span)[span_57](end_span).

## Core Systems
* **Financial Defense** - Emergency reserves, high-interest debt elimination, and essential insurance coverage[span_58](start_span)[span_58](end_span).
* **Cash Flow Systems** - Income routing, automated savings, and dynamic budgeting[span_59](start_span)[span_59](end_span).
* **Wealth Accumulation** - Asset allocation, index fund mechanics, and portfolio construction (including digital asset ETFs)[span_60](start_span)[span_60](end_span).
* **Tax Efficiency** - Account sequencing, tax-loss harvesting, and sheltering strategies[span_61](start_span)[span_61](end_span).
* **Retirement Runway** - Safe withdrawal rates, compounding math, and financial independence targets[span_62](start_span)[span_62](end_span).
* **Legacy Mechanics** - Beneficiary optimization, basic trusts, and wealth transfer[span_63](start_span)[span_63](end_span).

Select a system from the sidebar to begin building your plan[span_64](start_span)[span_64](end_span).
