# Cash Flow Systems

Willpower fails. Systems scale. Wealth is generated by the delta between what you earn and what you spend, but relying on manual budgeting every month leads to fatigue. You need an automated routing system[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).

## The Pay-Yourself-First Architecture
Instead of spending first and saving what is left, invert the process[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span). 

1. **The Sweep:** Set up an automatic transfer on the day your paycheck hits. Route a fixed percentage (target 20%+) immediately to your investment accounts and HYSA[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span)[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).
2. **The Buffer:** Leave 1-2 months of expenses in your primary checking account to prevent overdrafts[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span)[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span).
3. **Guilt-Free Spending:** Whatever remains in your checking account after the sweep and fixed bills is yours to spend without tracking every coffee[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span)[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).

## Income Routing Targets (The 50/30/20 Rule)
Use this as a baseline diagnostic tool:
* **50% Needs:** Housing, groceries, utilities, minimum debt payments[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span)[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span).
* **30% Wants:** Dining out, travel, entertainment[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span).
* **20% Future You:** Debt paydown (above minimums), investing, and saving. If your "Needs" exceed 50%, you cannot budget your way out of it—you have a structural housing or transportation cost problem[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span)[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span).
