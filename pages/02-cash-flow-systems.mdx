# Cash Flow Systems

Willpower fails. Systems scale. Wealth is generated by the delta between what you earn and what you spend, but relying on manual budgeting every month leads to fatigue. This framework replaces that with an automated routing system.

## The Pay-Yourself-First Architecture
Instead of spending first and saving what is left, invert the process. 

1. **The Sweep:** An automatic transfer runs on the day the paycheck lands, sending a fixed percentage straight to investment accounts and the HYSA. That percentage is the savings rate you choose; the framework's benchmark is 20% or more.
2. **The Buffer:** 1-2 months of expenses stay in the primary checking account to prevent overdrafts.
3. **Guilt-Free Spending:** Whatever remains in your checking account after the sweep and fixed bills is yours to spend without tracking every coffee.

## Income Routing Targets (The 50/30/20 Rule)

* **50% Needs:** Housing, groceries, utilities, minimum debt payments.
* **30% Wants:** Dining out, travel, entertainment.
* **20% Future You:** Debt paydown (above minimums), investing, and saving. If "Needs" exceed 50%, trimming wants rarely closes the gap; the cause is usually a structural housing or transportation cost.

## Combating Lifestyle Creep (The Holding Account)
High-income earners often suffer from Parkinson's Law of Money: expenses rise to meet income. A large balance sitting in checking tends to get spent.

Run your numbers through this diagnostic to see how efficiently you are capturing your career growth:

<LifestyleCreepDiagnoser />

* **The Solution:** An intermediate "Holding Account" (a separate checking account at a completely different bank). 
* **The Flow:** The paycheck is deposited directly into the Holding Account. From there, automated transfers push exactly what fixed bills and guilt-free spending need to primary checking, and route the rest directly to investments. The extra step keeps surplus cash out of sight.
