# Financial Defense

Offense wins games, but defense wins championships. In this framework, a financial moat against unexpected shocks comes before any capital goes into the markets.

## 1. The Emergency Reserve
The framework builds a cash buffer before investing long-term capital. Without one, an emergency during a downturn can force a sale of volatile assets at a loss.
* **The Target:** 3 to 6 months of absolute baseline living expenses.
* **The Vehicle:** A High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA) or a money market fund. This money is for liquidity, not growth, so the framework takes on no extra risk here for extra yield.

### Sizing the Moat
Not all emergencies are created equal. The reserve scales with structural risk:

<EmergencyReserveCalculator />

* **Lean toward 3 Months if:** You are a W-2 employee in a highly in-demand field, you are part of a dual-income household where both partners earn enough to cover the baseline, and you rent rather than own your home.
* **Lean toward 6+ Months if:** You are a freelancer or contractor with variable income, you are a single-income household, you own a home (where major repairs fall on you), or you have multiple dependents.

## 2. High-Interest Debt Elimination
Every dollar that pays down a 20%+ APR balance avoids that interest charge, while investment returns are never certain.

<DebtAvalancheSorter />

* **The Avalanche Method:** Debts are ranked by interest rate, highest to lowest. Every debt gets its minimum payment, and any extra cash goes to the highest-rate balance, so the most expensive debt is paid off first.
* **The Rule:** This framework treats any debt with an interest rate above 6% as a financial emergency, paid off before investing beyond the employer match.

## 3. Essential Insurance
Insurance covers catastrophic loss by transferring unrecoverable risk to an insurance company.
* **Life Insurance:** The framework follows Buy Term and Invest the Difference (BTID). For households with dependents, that means a 20- or 30-year term policy sized to replace the income the family would lose. Whole life and universal life policies carry higher premiums because they bundle insurance with an investment account; this framework keeps the two separate.
* **Disability Insurance:** Your ability to earn an income is your largest asset. The framework covers it with own-occupation long-term disability insurance.
