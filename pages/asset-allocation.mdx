# Asset Allocation

Strategic asset allocation is the primary driver of portfolio variance and long-term returns. 

## Core Principles

- **Risk Capacity vs. Tolerance:** Aligning mathematical ability to take risk with psychological willingness.
- **Equity-to-Fixed Income Ratios:** Establishing a baseline glide path.
- **Location Optimization:** Placing inefficient assets in tax-advantaged accounts.
