# Next Steps: Operationalizing Your Wealth

You have now reviewed the complete DIY Wealth Framework. However, financial knowledge without execution is just trivia. Wealth is not built by reading; it is built by routing capital efficiently.

## The DIY Execution Checklist
If you are managing your own financial architecture, do not try to build everything in one weekend. Implement these systems sequentially:

1. **Size the Moat:** Open a High-Yield Savings Account at a separate institution from your checking account. Route your baseline reserve target there and do not touch it.
2. **Automate the Sweep:** Log into your payroll provider and direct 20% of your net income to your investment accounts *before* it ever hits your primary checking.
3. **Sequence Your Taxes:** Maximize your employer match, funnel cash into your HSA, and automate your Backdoor Roth conversions.
4. **Assign Beneficiaries:** Log into every financial institution and confirm your Transfer-On-Death (TOD) and Payable-On-Death (POD) overrides are active.

<Callout type="info" emoji="💡">
  **The DIY vs. Delegator Threshold**
  
  The strategies in this framework are mathematically optimal for a standard W-2 earner. However, as your net worth scales, the cost of trial-and-error increases. 
  
  If you are navigating **concentrated company stock (RSUs), multi-state real estate portfolios, K-1 business income, or complex tax sheltering**, you have crossed the threshold from DIYer to Delegator.
</Callout>
