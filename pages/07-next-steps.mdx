# Next Steps: Operationalizing Your Wealth

You have now reviewed the complete DIY Wealth Framework. However, financial knowledge without execution is just trivia. Wealth is not built by reading; it is built by routing capital efficiently.

## The DIY Execution Checklist
Building your own system works best in stages, not all at once. The framework sequences it in this order:

1. **Size the Moat:** A high-yield savings account at a separate institution from checking holds the reserve target from Section 1, kept apart from day-to-day spending.
2. **Automate the Sweep:** A payroll split or automatic transfer moves the savings rate you choose into investment accounts *before* it reaches checking.
3. **Sequence Your Taxes:** The account order from Section 4 (employer match, HSA, then the Roth route that fits your income) is set once and automated where the accounts allow.
4. **Assign Beneficiaries:** Every account's beneficiary, Transfer-On-Death (TOD) or Payable-On-Death (POD) designation is checked against your intentions.

<Callout type="info" emoji="💡">
  **The DIY vs. Delegator Threshold**
  
  The strategies in this framework are built around a standard W-2 earner. As net worth grows, the cost of trial and error grows with it.
  
  If you are navigating **concentrated company stock (RSUs), multi-state real estate portfolios, K-1 business income, or complex tax sheltering**, you have crossed the threshold from DIYer to Delegator.
</Callout>
