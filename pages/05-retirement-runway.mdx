# Retirement Runway

Retirement is a mathematical equation, not an age. You achieve Financial Independence (FI) when the passive income from your portfolio exceeds your living expenses.

## The 25x Rule (The Target)
To determine your FI number, calculate your projected annual expenses in retirement and multiply by 25.
* *Example:* If you need $80,000 a year to live comfortably, your FI target is $2,000,000.

## The 4% Rule (The Execution)
The 25x rule is the inverse of the 4% Safe Withdrawal Rate. Historical studies of U.S. market data found that a 4% first-year withdrawal, adjusted for inflation each year after, lasted 30 years in most of the past periods tested. Past results do not guarantee future ones.

## Sequence of Return Risk (The Bond Tent)
The biggest threat to the 4% Rule is experiencing a severe market crash in the first few years of your retirement. Withdrawing living expenses while the portfolio is down means selling assets at depressed prices, and those shares are no longer there to recover.

* **The Solution:** A "Bond Tent." In the 3 to 5 years leading up to retirement, 2 to 3 years' worth of living expenses accumulates in cash or short-term bond ETFs.
* **The Execution:** If the stock market crashes in year one of retirement, the framework stops selling equities and draws living expenses from the Bond Tent instead, giving the equity portfolio time to recover before standard withdrawals resume.

## Compounding Mechanics
Time in the market is the heaviest lever you can pull.
* A $10,000 investment growing at an annualized 7% doubles roughly every 10 years.
* At that same illustrative 7%, a 10-year delay means one fewer doubling, so reaching the same ending balance takes roughly twice the starting amount.
