# Legacy Mechanics

If you die without a plan, the state has one for you—and it is slow, public, and expensive. Basic estate planning ensures your wealth transfers exactly how you intend.

## 1. Beneficiary Designations (The Override)
Accounts with named beneficiaries bypass probate entirely. This is the fastest way to transfer wealth.
* **Action:** Log into every bank, brokerage, and 401(k) account today. Ensure you have named Primary and Contingent beneficiaries.
* **POD/TOD:** Add a Payable-On-Death (POD) or Transfer-On-Death (TOD) designation to your checking, savings, and taxable brokerage accounts.
* *Warning:* Beneficiary designations trump whatever is written in your will. Keep them updated after major life events (marriage, divorce, children).

## 2. The Core Documents
Every adult needs these three documents, regardless of net worth:
* **Last Will and Testament:** Dictates the distribution of assets not covered by TOD/POD, and crucially, names guardians for minor children.
* **Durable Financial Power of Attorney:** Appoints someone to handle your finances, pay bills, and manage assets if you are incapacitated but still alive.
* **Advance Healthcare Directive:** Names a proxy to make medical decisions on your behalf and outlines your wishes for end-of-life care.

## 3. The Trust Threshold (Will vs. Trust)
A Last Will and Testament guarantees your estate will go through probate court. Probate is public record, costs time, and incurs legal fees. A Revocable Living Trust holds your assets directly, allowing you to bypass probate entirely.
