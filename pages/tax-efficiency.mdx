# Tax Strategies

Tax efficiency is about controlling what you keep, not just what you earn.

## Core Concepts

- **Asset Location:** Placing yield-heavy assets in tax-advantaged accounts.
- **Tax-Loss Harvesting:** Capturing paper losses to offset realized capital gains.
- **Strategic Conversions:** Utilizing low-income years for Roth conversions.
